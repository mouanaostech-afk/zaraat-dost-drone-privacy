Zaraat Dost Drone: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Zaraat Dost Drone app (Android package pk.zaraatdost.drone) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The app is operated by ZARAAT DOST (PRIVATE) LIMITED ("Zaraat Dost", "we"), 7th Floor, Aiwan-e-Iqbal, Pakistan.

1. Who uses the app

  • Pilots: drone spray operators who record sprays and field visits and carry out booked sprays.
  • Drone desk: Zaraat Dost staff who quote rates, hand bookings to pilots and review records.

There is no public sign-up. Accounts are created by Zaraat Dost. The app is for adults (18+) working with Zaraat Dost.

The app also shows and records information about farmers (growers) whose fields are sprayed. Farmers do not use this app; this policy also explains how their information is handled in it.

3. Information about farmers shown or recorded in the app

To find the right grower and field, the app looks up records Zaraat Dost already holds, including the land survey and the mills' grower records. Depending on the record, a pilot or the desk may see or enter:

  • Name, father's name, CNIC, grower/mill code and mobile number;
  • Village (mauza), circle and mill, and field boundaries and area;
  • Spray bookings and records: crop, pest problem, pesticide, acres sprayed, date, rate and amount, and notes.

We use this only to arrange and record drone sprays and to inform the farmer (for example by SMS or in the Zaraat Dost farmer app when a spray is quoted, booked or done). Pilots see only what they need for their work.

4. What stays on the phone

  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the phone's secure storage (Keystore). Fingerprint or face unlock, if you turn it on, is checked by the phone itself; we never receive it.
  • Records saved without internet are kept on the phone until they upload, then removed.
  • Recently searched growers, your language choice and recently loaded screens are cached on the phone for speed. Signing out clears your account's cached data.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising. The app has no ads and no analytics or advertising trackers. We use these service providers only to run the app:

6. Security

All data between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is limited by role: a pilot sees his own work, the desk sees all drone work. Sessions expire, and a lost phone's session can be ended by the desk by resetting the password or deactivating the account.

7. How long we keep it

Spray and visit records are kept as Zaraat Dost's business records. Password reset codes expire in 10 minutes; sign-in sessions expire within 7 days of inactivity. When an account is closed, it is deactivated; work records already made stay with Zaraat Dost as business records.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Location: you can deny or remove the location permission in Android settings; marking a field by walking will then not work.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in Android settings.
  • Access, correction, deletion: pilots, desk staff and farmers may ask to see, correct or delete their information, or to close an account, by writing to info@zaraatdost.com. We will reply as soon as we can. Some records may be kept where the law or an open matter requires it.

9. Children

The app is not for children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 through it.
